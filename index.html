ALL YOU NEED TO KNOW ABOUT APEXPAY WALLET

APEXPAY Wallet – Overview
APEXPAY Wallet is a digital earning and wallet platform designed to give users a simple way to participate in daily earning activities, manage their balances, and request withdrawals to supported Nigerian bank accounts.

⚙️ How the App Works

👤 Registration: Create your account using your name, phone number, and required bank details.
⛏️ Mining: Click the Mine button daily to activate your earning session.
💰 Earnings: Earn according to the current mining rate and the conditions available on APEXPAY.
🚀 Miner Upgrade: Upgrade your miner where available to unlock the earning features associated with the selected level.
🏦 Withdrawals: Request payment directly to your supported Nigerian bank account when you meet the applicable withdrawal requirements.

💚 Daily earning opportunities

📱 How to Use APEXPAY WALLET

Step 1: Create your APEXPAY account.
Step 2: Click Mine to start your daily earning session.
Step 3: Monitor your earnings from your wallet dashboard.
Step 4: Go to Withdraw when eligible and request your payment.
Step 5: Upgrade your miner to access the earning features available at that level.
