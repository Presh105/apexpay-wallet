Welcome to APEXPAY

APEXPAY Wallet is a digital asset platform designed to help you grow your wealth effortlessly. Our cloud-based system allows you to mine digital funds directly from your smartphone without draining your battery or data.

How Mining Works

Click the MINE button on your dashboard to start the automated process. New users are granted a free mining capacity (₦96,000) per session. Once this limit is reached, you must withdraw your funds before you can mine again.

How to Withdraw

To ensure security and prevent bot accounts, a one-time Withdrawal Code is required to cash out. You can securely purchase this code inside the app by sending the fee to the official Admin account. Once approved, your 6-digit code will be issued.

Account Upgrades

Want to mine beyond the free limit? You can upgrade your account to unlock higher mining capacities. Click on MORE MINES on your dashboard to contact the Admin for upgrade packages.
